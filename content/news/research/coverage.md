## Discovery coverage

## Selection decisions

Include substantive local research, engineering or production even when headquarters are elsewhere.
Proxima Fusion's 2025 announcement explicitly identifies engineering at PSI near Zurich; its earlier round remains held until that local connection is established for the earlier date.
Do not treat a holding company's canton, a founder's ETH degree or an investor's Swiss address as proof of a local technical operation.
Aurealis, Berlin Heals and InnoMedica therefore remain outside the published backfill pending stronger local evidence.

Exclude grants and awards, sub-1M announcements, undisclosed rounds, secondary share sales, fundraising targets and ongoing campaigns without a qualifying completed investment.
Exclude general retail, food brands, marketplaces, real estate brokerage, insurance products and consulting when no substantial technology product is established.
This excludes candidates such as Fabas, Digt, maison.work, Calingo, CarbonPool and aighty-twenty from this pass; it does not exclude actual food biotechnology such as Food Brewer and Sallea.

Preserve original names in historical headlines, including Swiss-Mile and Spiden; current company links may use RIVR and Liom.
Use company announcements as reader destinations when available, otherwise current company homepages.
Research links remain in the ledgers instead of crowding the public cards.
