## Editorial and verification rules

Read `content/news/README.md`, `content/news/research/non-funding.md`, `coverage.md`, `sources.md`, and the existing Apertus brief, and searched the news tree for candidate names and technologies.
No matching existing brief was found for the recommended research candidates below in that local search.
The IBM quantum-system plan is already in the non-funding provenance shortlist, and the original Apertus release is already covered.
Production was not queried; deduplication is against the local files and the user's production context, not a claim to have audited live production.
Categories below are editorial descriptions, not assertions about accepted importer values or existing topic slugs.
Recommended titles state what changed and avoid promotional superlatives.
Announcement dates and underlying publication dates are separated, so a newer university post does not silently reset an older result's date.
High confidence means an opened original announcement and, where available, an opened paper or artifact support the central claim; it does not mean independent experimental replication.
Holds and discovery-only leads are not verified recommendations.

### ETH develops OLED materials compatible with chip lithography

- Original publication: 2026-09-16 for the Nature paper; ETH announcement dated 2026-09-23.
- Source title: Tiny OLED pixels can be manufactured like microchips.
- Source: [ETH announcement](https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2026/09/tiny-oled-pixels-can-be-manufactured-like-microchips.html), opened and read.
- Primary corroboration: [Nature paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11042-0), opened; publication date, abstract, demonstrations, and author affiliations available.
- Summary: ETH researchers developed light-emitting polymers that withstand lithographic processing and demonstrated patterned, electrically powered OLEDs.
  The work brings organic emitters closer to chip fabrication, but does not yet deliver an independently addressable display.
- Zurich connection: Chih-Jen Shih's chemistry group and Hua Wang's electronics group at ETH Zurich carried out the work with international collaborators.
- Category: Research; microelectronics and materials.
- Recommendation: Strong lead for launch coverage, with the demonstrated manufacturing method in the headline.
- Confidence: High.
- Qualifications: The microscopic multicolour picture is a fluorescence demonstration, not an electrically driven screen; the separate electrically powered demonstration is a 1 by 2.4 mm ETH logo.
  Do not present nanoscale fluorescent features as a finished nanoscale OLED display or claim commercial manufacturing readiness.

### ETH demonstrates a freezing method for textured whole legumes

- Original publication and ETH announcement: 2026-09-01, both verified on opened pages.
- Source title: Food innovation in your kitchen: Giving peas, beans and lentils a new bite.
- Source: [ETH announcement](https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2026/09/food-innovation-in-your-kitchen-giving-peas-beans-and-lentils-a-new-bite.html).

### WSL and ETH reconstruct the Blatten avalanche using seismic data

- Original publication: 2026-09-07 for the paper; WSL announcement dated 2026-09-15.
- Source title: Erdbebensensoren enthuellen, was beim Fels- und Eissturz von Blatten geschah.
- Source: [WSL announcement](https://www.wsl.ch/de/news/erdbebensensoren-enthuellen-was-beim-fels-und-eissturz-von-blatten-geschah/), opened and read.
- Primary corroboration: [Communications Earth & Environment paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-026-03983-1), opened, including date, affiliations, and data/code availability statements.
- Summary: A WSL-led team combined seismic recordings, terrain observations, and simulations to reconstruct the 2025 Blatten rock-and-ice avalanche and identify how strongly reduced friction shaped its motion.
  The paper supplies a reproducible analysis approach and links derived research data and code for further hazard modelling.
- Zurich connection: Lead authors Jiahui Kang and Fabian Walter list WSL Birmensdorf affiliations, with additional ETH Zurich geotechnical and glaciology contributors.
- Category: Research; simulation, geospatial data, and natural-hazard technology.
- Recommendation: Strong candidate, especially for an applied-research and open-artifact angle.
- Confidence: High for the result and publication date; medium for artifact availability because linked archives were not downloaded or executed.
- Artifact pointer: The paper links [derived data and analysis code on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21511788) and public Swiss Seismological Service waveforms.
- Qualifications: Some original high-resolution terrain models cannot be redistributed; do not call every input open.
  This is a retrospective reconstruction of an event on 2025-05-28, not an operational warning system or a new disaster report.
