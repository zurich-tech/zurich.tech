subtitle: |-
    Namespace has raised USD 42M to expand its infrastructure for building and testing software. The platform runs development environments, GitHub Actions and build workloads on its own server racks, with support for Linux, Windows and macOS.

    The funding will support product development and additional data-centre capacity, including a larger fleet of Mac hardware for iOS and macOS builds. Namespace reports more than 1,000 customers and eightfold revenue growth over the past year. The round brings its total funding to USD 65M, seven months after its Series A.
