title: "FLUX-mimic learns to sort car parts"
subtitle: |-
    Mimic and BMW researchers tested FLUX-mimic on automotive tasks including component assembly, cable routing and sorting deformable foam parts into trays.
    The model combines a pretrained FLUX 3 video backbone with an action model that generates robot movements, without needing to generate a complete video during operation.

    In the foam-part sorting experiment, a Franka robot succeeded in 95% of 40 trials after eight hours of action-labelled demonstrations.
    That figure excludes earlier backbone pretraining: the project collected nearly 296 hours of industrial recordings across 31 tasks, alongside the backbone's broader training data.
    The results demonstrate controlled research tasks with separately trained robot policies, not general-purpose factory autonomy.
