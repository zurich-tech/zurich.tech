title: "Cotierra raises EUR 2.6M"
subtitle: |-
    Cotierra raised EUR 2.6M to expand decentralised biochar production from pilots into repeatable, multi-year commercial deployments, initially in coffee supply chains.
    The Zurich company combines reactors that turn agricultural residues into biochar near their source with software to operate, monitor and verify production.

    Its system tracks production and application of biochar, which returns carbon-rich material to agricultural soils.
    The financing announcement describes plans to expand across regions and agricultural supply chains; it does not quantify achieved carbon removal or crop-yield improvements.
