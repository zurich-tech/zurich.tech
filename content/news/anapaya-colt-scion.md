title: "Colt launches Anapaya-based SCION networking across three continents"
subtitle: |-
    Colt launched a fully managed SCION networking service across Europe, North America and Asia using Zurich-based Anapaya's technology.
    Commercial availability follows a two-year proof of concept, including testing with financial-sector organisations.
    The service lets customers define, audit and enforce the paths their traffic takes, giving them control over routing for sensitive or regulated communications.

    Colt offers two configurations: SCION private connects customers to existing private ecosystems and extranets, while SCION Global provides a WAN for an organisation's backbone network.
    The release describes security and resilience benefits but provides no quantified performance benchmark or service-availability measurements.
