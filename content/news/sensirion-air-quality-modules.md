title: "Sensirion releases SEN68 and SEN69C air-quality modules"
subtitle: |-
    Sensirion released the SEN68 and SEN69C modules for indoor air-quality equipment, combining particulate sensing with humidity, temperature, VOC, NOx and formaldehyde measurements.
    SEN69C adds CO2 sensing.
    Both use the new SPS6x miniaturised MEMS particulate-sensing component to fit multiple measurements into a compact module.

    The modules and SEK-SEN69C evaluation kit are available through distributors.
    The kit exposes the measurements and onboard algorithms for prototyping, but requires a separate SEK-SensorBridge to operate.
    These are components for products such as room sensors and air purifiers, not a finished building-monitoring installation.
