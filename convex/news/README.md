# News

Published news and drafts live in Convex and are maintained through the website editor.
The repository does not store a second content catalogue.

## Selection

Cover significant fundraising, demonstrated technology, substantial product releases and important research with a concrete result.
Require a substantive Zurich engineering, research or production connection, not merely a registered address or founder affiliation.
For fundraising, require a disclosed round of at least CHF, EUR or USD 1M; distinguish new funding from cumulative totals, extensions, debt and grants.
Exclude routine acquisitions, partnerships, awards, office openings and unspecified investments.

## Editing

Use a concrete headline and one to three substantive paragraphs in your own words.
Explain what changed, what the technology does and what was demonstrated; preserve material limitations and distinguish planned work from operational results.
Use the original announcement date and currency, a direct primary-source link, relevant entity references and specific existing tags.
Keep the feed summary concise rather than repeating the full story.
Do not credit external briefs to team members as authored articles.

## Media

Include relevant original media: a product, experiment, demo or verified team photograph.
Use playable video for demonstrations, with a still image for email and social previews.
Put attribution in the media caption, not the article body.
Do not use stock photographs, unrelated images or logos as cover images.
